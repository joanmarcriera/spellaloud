How to install one (iPhone, iPad, Android, Windows, Mac, Chromebook)

The app reads words in an English (UK) voice that is installed on the device. Nothing is sent anywhere. Without one, each word needs your child's own recording. Install the voice, then reload this page.

The buttons below open the device's own settings. If nothing happens, follow the steps instead.

iPhone and iPad

No link: Apple does not let a web page open Settings. Settings > Accessibility > Spoken Content > Voices > English > English (UK). Pick a voice and tap the download arrow.

Android

Open text-to-speech settings

Settings > System > Languages & input > Text-to-speech output. Choose the Google engine, open its settings, then Install voice data > English (United Kingdom).

Windows

Open speech settings

Settings > Time & language > Language & region > Add a language > English (United Kingdom), with Speech selected. Then Settings > Time & language > Speech > Manage voices > Add voices.

macOS

Open Accessibility settings

System Settings > Accessibility > Spoken Content > System Voice > Manage Voices > English (United Kingdom), then download one.

ChromeOS

No link: Chrome cannot open its settings from a web page. Settings > Accessibility > Text-to-Speech > Voices. Under English choose English (United Kingdom) and let it download.

Still says there is no British voice?

Some browsers (Chrome on Linux, for example) only offer online voices. The app does not use those, because they send each word to the internet. Use a device from the list above, or record the words in your own voice.
