My words disappeared

Words are stored in your browser on this device. Clearing website data, using a private window or switching browser hides them. If you made a backup file, open the app, choose the backup option and import that file. To avoid it happening again, export a backup now and then and keep the file somewhere safe, such as Files or an email to yourself.

There is no sound on my iPhone

Check that the side switch is not on silent and the volume is up, then tap a word to hear it: iPhones only play sound after a tap. If a word still stays silent, open Settings, Accessibility, Spoken Content, Voices, and download an English (United Kingdom) voice.

Where is my data?

On your own device only. Words, recordings and scores never reach us, so we cannot see or restore them. The one thing we hold is the email address of anyone who joined the waiting list, described on the privacy page.

Contact us, and refunds
