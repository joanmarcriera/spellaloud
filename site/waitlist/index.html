The spelling list, read aloud, so you do not have to.

Is your child given spellings every week, and you are not sure how to say the words? Spellaloud is a small app, being built now, that reads them in a British voice. It is not released yet; join the list below and I will tell you when it is ready.

  1. Type the school's spelling list (photographing it is planned, not promised).
  2. Your child hears each word, writes it in their book, and ticks what they got right.
  3. See which words are tricky and practise those.
  • No ads. No tracking. No accounts. Everything stays on your own phone or tablet.
  • Works offline once installed. Made for children in English primary schools, Reception to Year 6.
  • Made by a parent who needed it for his own two children.

Is this right for us?

Will my child's school list already be in it?
Not built in: schools set their own lists, so you type the week's words once and the app reads them aloud in a British voice. Photographing the sheet is planned, not promised. Source
Will my lists survive an update?
Words and recordings live in your browser's own storage on the device, while an update only replaces the app's code, so your lists stay put. Clearing website data does remove them, which is why the app reminds you to keep a backup file. Read more
What if I lose or change the phone?
Because nothing is held on our side, the way to move is a backup file: export it on the old device and import it on the new one. Keep that file somewhere safe, such as Files or an email to yourself. Read more
Is it a subscription?
Practising is free and there is no recurring charge. The only extra we propose is an optional one-off backup pack at 2.99 pounds, not on sale yet. Read more
What happens to my child's data?
Words, recordings and scores stay on your own device and never reach us. The only thing we hold is the email address of anyone who joins the waiting list. Read more
What if it does not suit us, can I get a refund?
If you buy the optional backup pack, the draft terms say you can email within 30 days and ask for a refund without giving a reason. The terms are a draft, pending Marc's confirmation. Read more
