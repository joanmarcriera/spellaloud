Terms, in plain English

Draft, last updated 2026-10-25. Proposed, Marc to confirm.

What is free

Practising spellings is free: typing a list, hearing the words, writing them down, ticking what your child got right and seeing progress. Saving a backup file of your words and recordings, and restoring it on another device, is free too (proposed, pending Marc). There are no ads, no accounts and no trackers. The app keeps your child's words and recordings only on your own device.

The optional backup pack

We propose a small optional extra, a backup pack, as a one-off payment of £2.99. It is not on sale yet. If it is, the price shown at checkout is the price you pay, and the practice part stays free.

Refunds

If you buy the backup pack and it is not right for you, email support@spellaloud.invalid within 30 days of buying and ask for a refund. You do not need to give a reason. We will process the refund within 3 business days of your email.

Using the app

The app is a practice aid for parents and children. It is not affiliated with any school or with the Department for Education, and the word lists you type are your own. We do our best to keep it working, but it comes without a guarantee, and nothing here limits the rights you have under UK consumer law.

Questions

See the help page, the privacy page, or email support@spellaloud.invalid. We aim to reply within two working days.
