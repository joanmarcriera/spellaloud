# UK statutory spelling lists: provenance

`lists.json` (schema 1) is generated by `tools/extract_statutory_lists.py` from the text of:

- **Title:** The national curriculum in England - English Appendix 1: Spelling (Department for Education, Sept 2013)
- **Landing page:** https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/national-curriculum-in-england-english-programmes-of-study
- **PDF:** https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/media/5a7ccc06ed915d63cc65ce61/English_Appendix_1_-_Spelling.pdf
- **Retrieved:** 2026-10-23 (`pdftotext -layout`)
- **Licence:** Open Government Licence v3.0. The PDF's own last page says "(c) Crown copyright 2013. You may re-use
  this information (excluding logos) free of charge in any format or medium, under the terms of the Open Government
  Licence." The GOV.UK publication page also names OGL v3.0.
- **Attribution used on our pages:** "Contains public sector information licensed under the Open Government Licence
  v3.0." plus "Not affiliated with or endorsed by the Department for Education."

## Counts recorded from the source

| List | Entries | Statutory? |
|---|---|---|
| Year 1 common exception words | 45 | No: printed as non-statutory example words ("and/or others according to the programme used") |
| Year 2 common exception words | 64 | No: same; `children` is included "because of its relationship with `child`" |
| Years 3 and 4 word list | 100 | Yes |
| Years 5 and 6 word list | 100 | Yes |

`official` is the entry as printed, minus the footnote asterisk on `conscience*`, `conscious*` and `children*`.
`forms` are the words a child practises: `accident(ally)` gives `accident`, `accidentally`;
`equip (–ped, –ment)` gives `equip`, `equipped`, `equipment`; `criticise (critic + ise)` gives `criticise`.
The Year 1 and 2 rows are lifted from the "Common exception words" rows of the Year 1 and Year 2 tables only. The
"Homophones" rows are not part of these lists.

Order: the Years 3-4 and 5-6 tables are alphabetical down each printed column; `lists.json` keeps that order. GOV.UK itself
prints `increase` before `important` in Years 3-4 (kept as printed, guarded by a test).

| Gorse Hall Primary (Tameside), Common Exception Words Checklist: https://gorsehall.tameside.sch.uk/wp-content/uploads/2021/09/Common-Exception-Words-Checklist.pdf | Y1, Y2 | All 45 Y1 and 64 Y2 words are present in the checklist; no discrepancy found in that direction. Conclusion: no change to `lists.json` from the cross-check. Two small school copies are incomplete, which is a reason
to cite GOV.UK directly. Not independently checked: the reverse direction for Y1/Y2 (extra words in the school
checklist beyond our 109), because the checklist also holds Year 2 sections laid out in a table.
